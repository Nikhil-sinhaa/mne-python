:meth:`mne.io.Raw.plot` and :func:`mne.viz.plot_raw` gained an ``overlay`` argument (and ``overlay_colors``) to draw other :class:`~mne.io.Raw` objects, such as a filtered or otherwise cleaned copy, on top of the traces in the same window, aligned by acquisition time. Scrolling, zooming and annotating work as usual, and annotation edits are also applied to the overlaid objects. This is supported by the matplotlib and Qt browser backends (the latter from mne-qt-browser 0.8 on), by `Nikhil Sinha`_.
